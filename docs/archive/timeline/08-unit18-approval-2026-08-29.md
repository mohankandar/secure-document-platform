# Unit 18 Approval — Confidence & Exception Processing

**Status:** APPROVED 2026-08-29

Unit 18 was verified on the real local stack with Java 21, PostgreSQL, Ollama `gemma3:12b`, and Tesseract. `mvn clean verify`, frontend type-check, and Vite build all passed after two minimal corrections found during verification.

## Verified behavior

- Focused review queue returns only unresolved `DETECTED` findings; auto-confirmed findings do not re-enter human review.
- `REVIEWER` role gate and tenant-scoped review queries are enforced.
- Confirm/dismiss resolve the finding and the last unresolved item automatically resumes redaction, verification, package-atomic delivery, and completion.
- Unlocated findings cannot be confirmed without trusted geometry; the system never guesses coordinates.
- Transient AI and delivery failures use bounded selective retry; non-retryable failures do not loop.
- Technical failures remain technical and do not become `PENDING_REVIEW`.
- Structured dependency/reason/retryability/attempt detail remains safe and excludes sensitive values/provider exception text.
- Unit 16 digital and Unit 17 scanned/OCR regressions both passed.

## Verification corrections retained

1. `ApplicationServiceConfig.java`: added the missing `TriggerPackageProcessingUseCase` import needed by the focused-review bean.
2. `JpaFindingRepositoryAdapter.java`: `saveAll` now reuses the version-aware `save` path so confirm -> automatic resume -> redaction does not collide with JPA `@Version` state.

## Accepted limitations

- Retry remains intentionally small and synchronous for V1 construction.
- No durable delayed retry scheduler yet.
- No dedicated indexed technical-exception projection yet.
- Secondary AI failover is supported but was not runtime-exercised because no real secondary provider was configured.
- Unit 17 duplicate-OCR remains a deferred performance optimization.
