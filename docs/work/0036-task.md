## Backend
1. **Per-destination worker limit holds under concurrency** (0035 finding 1). In `JdbcPipelineJobRepository` (`CLAIM_NEXT_SQL`), two workers claiming at the same moment must not both run jobs for one destination beyond `workers-per-destination`. Use a lock per (entity, destination) while claiming, for example `pg_advisory_xact_lock` on a hash of the pair, or lock the destination's state row; choose and say why. Keep `SKIP LOCKED` behaviour for different destinations so they don't wait on each other.
2. **Queue order never depends on the clock** (finding 2). Migration **045**: add `queue_seq BIGINT` to `pipeline_jobs`, filled from a sequence on insert, existing rows numbered in their current `created_at, id` order, NOT NULL afterwards, index for the claim query. Claim and "resume in order" use `queue_seq`, not `created_at`. Due times (`next_attempt_at`) still use time; that's correct.
3. **One destination down raises the alert** (answer 1). `allDestinationsDown` (in `ExportJobService`) is true when every destination of the entity is paused for a failed check, **including when there is only one**. The pause stays; the alert clears when any destination resumes.

## Screens
4. **Timings come from the settings** (answer 4). Every sentence that names a probe interval or a retry span takes it from the real settings, through the API, not fixed text: the case panel ("checks it every 5 minutes"), the Destinations note, the IT card ("retried over about 17 hours and rebuilt once"). Round in plain words (minutes under an hour, hours above). Give the screen what it needs in the existing export status responses; no new endpoint unless needed (say why).
5. **Cases page: an Exporting tile.** Tiles become New · Redacting · Review · Approval · **Exporting** · Exported · Stopped. A case whose chip says "Exporting · …" counts under Exporting, not Redacting. Same colour family as the Exporting chip.
6. **Support › Destinations, Waiting column:** count each state on its own: "2 ready", "1 waiting for you", "2 need IT" (Needs IT in the red style). Never count Needs IT as ready.
7. **IT card wording:** when the destination passes its health check, the card says **"Cases can't be written to Local export folder"** style wording ("<n> cases can't be delivered to <destination> since <time>"), not "Destination can't be reached". When the check fails, the paused wording stays. List the cases by their **case number** (the name shown on the Cases list), not the internal id; more than 5 → first 5 and "and <n> more".
